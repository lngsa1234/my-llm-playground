HotpotQA mini corpus attribution

This teaching subset is derived from the HotpotQA development distractor split:
https://hotpotqa.github.io/

HotpotQA: A Dataset for Diverse, Explainable Multi-hop Question Answering
Zhilin Yang et al., EMNLP 2018
https://aclanthology.org/D18-1259/

this directory are a derived subset; retain this notice and comply with the

This lab selected the first 50 rows returned by the Hugging Face HotpotQA
validation/distractor dataset API on 2026-10-04. For each question, it retains
the gold supporting Wikipedia articles and two supplied distractor articles.
The five unanswerable benchmark questions are original teaching prompts and
are not part of HotpotQA.
