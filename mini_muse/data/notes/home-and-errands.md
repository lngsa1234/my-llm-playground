## Dumplings (Grandma's recipe)
Filling: 500 g ground pork, 2 cups finely chopped napa cabbage (salted and squeezed), 3 spring onions,
1 tablespoon grated ginger, 2 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 tablespoon sesame oil.
Makes about 50 dumplings. Boil for 6 to 7 minutes, until they float, then 1 more minute.
