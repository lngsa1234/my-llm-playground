# Agent Engineering Project Pool

8-Week Peer Learning Circle | Choose a project. Build with a team. Demo your agent.

Starting no later than Week 5, we'll form small teams of 2–3 members and begin working on a project together. Each team will choose a project, define its scope, and work toward a functional MVP and final demo by Week 8. 🚀

These project ideas below are AI-generated starting points, waiting for you to bring them to life! Feel free to expand on an existing idea, tailor it to your interests or work, or propose something entirely new. Share your vision and find teammates who are excited to build it with you!

If you have your own project idea, feel free to add it to the project pool! Share a brief description so other members with similar interests can connect with you and form a team. The goal is to build something meaningful, learn together, and create a project you can showcase! 🚀

> Team rosters are kept in the circle's Notion workspace and are not published here.

## 2. AI Trading Research Agent

**Tag:** Finance and Investment

> 🖼️ *Architecture sketch* — [view in Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/3e5dbc95f92c804f9107febfbbd2c970)
